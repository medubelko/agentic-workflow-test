# Koan

A novice asked the master, "When the bell rings, where does the sound go?"

The master said nothing, but pointed to the novice's ear.

"There?" asked the novice.

The master shook his head and pointed to the space between them.

The novice stood puzzled.

"Ring the bell again," said the master.

"But the bell is not here," said the novice.

"Exactly," said the master.
